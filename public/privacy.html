OmniChat Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

OmniChat ("we", "our", or "the application") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when using the OmniChat web application.

1. Information We Collect and Access

OmniChat is designed with a privacy-first, decentralized architecture:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in using Google OAuth, we access your public profile name, email address, and avatar to identify your user profile within the application.
  • Google Drive Storage Access: OmniChat requests permission to create and manage files specifically created by OmniChat (using the drive.file scope). We access only your OmniChat_Data folder to store chats, messages, and attachments. We do not access, read, or modify any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

2. How Your Data is Stored (Decentralized & Private)

Unlike traditional messaging apps, OmniChat does not operate a central database or private server that stores your personal messages:

  • All message histories, contact aliases, audio voice notes, and media attachments are saved directly inside your own private Google Drive storage.
  • When you participate in a chat with another user, data synchronization occurs directly through shared Google Drive permissions granted by you.

3. Third-Party Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer your personal data or messages to any third parties or advertising networks. Your data remains solely between you, the participants you choose to invite, and Google Drive services.

4. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

OmniChat's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Deletion and Revocation

You can revoke OmniChat's access to your Google Account at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions. You can also delete your chat files and media permanently directly from your Google Drive folder at any time.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via GitHub or email.
